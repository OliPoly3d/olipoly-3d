Before you submit

A clear, quote-first process.

1. Share the beginning

Tell OliPoly the goal, quantity, desired timing, and anything you already have. Files, photos, and details help, but do not need to be polished.

2. Expect a review

OliPoly typically responds within 1–2 business days with questions, recommendations, or quote details.

3. Review the quote

Pricing can reflect size, material, print time, quantity, complexity, design/CAD, and finishing. Nothing moves into production until you accept the quote.

4. Make it real

Typical completion is approximately 1–2 weeks after quote approval. Complex design, quantity, materials, or other requirements may extend the timing.

Useful details

What the quote may address.

  • Normal design help may be included; substantial CAD/design work may add a disclosed fee.
  • Special materials or significant project-specific supplies may require a deposit. Once ordered, that supplies deposit is non-refundable.
  • Pickup is available in Aurora, Ohio; shipping is available anywhere in the United States; limited local delivery may be arranged.
  • Changes after approval are welcome to discuss, but may affect price, timing, or scope and may require updated approval.
  • Your supplied materials and pre-existing intellectual property remain yours or the appropriate owner's. OliPoly-created source/design files do not automatically transfer with the finished object; a source-file transfer may be negotiated separately.
