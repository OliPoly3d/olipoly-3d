How the month-end plan works

It includes recorded transactions, unrecorded income and bills, and the unspent part of each category target. Credit payment plans use their due dates. Legacy everyday-card payoff settings reserve unplanned debt at month-end. Future variable spending is charged to each category’s chosen cash account. Month-end planning does not predict the exact day that variable spending happens.

Credit interest is an estimate using entered APRs; missing APRs stay unknown. Unknown bills and missing budget targets still need your input. Protected savings remain separate.

Account terms & promotion

Use the current card agreement and statement. Minimums may change each statement. Interest estimates use APR / 365 and post at calendar month-end; statement cycles, grace periods, payment allocation, fees and issuer rounding may differ. Payments are estimated against higher-rate balances first. Only check the grace-period setting if your issuer confirms purchases currently qualify. Promotional balance interest still follows its own APR. Deferred-interest offers and cash advances are not modeled.
