How the month-end plan works

It includes recorded transactions, unrecorded income and bills, and the unspent part of each category target. Everyday-card balances selected in settings are planned to be paid by month end. Future variable spending is charged to each category’s chosen cash account. Month-end planning does not predict the exact day that variable spending happens.

Unknown bills, missing targets and interest after promotions are not included. Protected savings remain separate.
