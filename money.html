How the month-end plan works

It includes recorded transactions, unrecorded income and bills, and the unspent part of each category target. Credit payment plans use their due dates. Legacy everyday-card payoff settings reserve unplanned debt at month-end. Future variable spending is charged to each category’s chosen cash account. Category timing distributes the unspent allowance for planning; actual purchase dates replace those estimates. Daily lows assume debits settle before deposits on the same day.

Credit interest is an estimate using entered APRs; missing APRs stay unknown. Unknown bills and missing budget targets still need your input. Protected savings remain separate.

Review & reconcile
Finish / close the selected month

Close a past month after verifying cash balances, resolving pending entries and recording or skipping expectations. Closed months protect saved transactions, opening balances and monthly budget inputs in Money. They do not lock your bank or provide accounting certification. Later corrections can be dated in the current month with a note referencing the closed month. Recorded balances carry forward normally. Unspent allowances and later corrections are not silently moved.

Confirmed inputs, estimates and decisions

Confirmed means you supplied or checked the input. Estimated means it is an intentional planning assumption. Needs a decision keeps an unresolved item visible. Review labels do not fill missing rates, balances or bills; use their editors to enter those details.

Record each card purchase with its category and card account. To pay the card, choose Transfer / card payment, paid from checking, with the card as destination. The payment changes both balances; the original purchases keep their categories.

Merchant refunds and friend repayments reduce the original categories in the receipt month. A closed purchase month stays unchanged. Enter only the refunded share.

Savings purpose · optional

For an expense paid from a savings bucket, choosing its purpose selects a separate Savings category. Keep that category to spend saved money without reducing the regular monthly allowance too. A transfer out of savings reduces the bucket without counting as an expense. For card purchases funded by a bucket, record the real savings-to-checking transfer with that bucket, then use its Savings category for the purchase and record the card payoff as a transfer.
